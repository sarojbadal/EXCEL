## insights 
             - The highest shales month of cloth store is march 
             -around 92% of products are successfully deliverd 
             - Women are more likely to buy compared to men (~65%)
             - Maharashtra,Karnataka and Utter Pradesh are the top 3 states (~35%)
             - Adult age group (30-49)is mas contributing (~50%)
             -Amazon,Flipkart and Myntra channels are max contributing (~80%)
   


          
             
# final conclusion to improve the cloth store shales :
 - Target women costumers of age group (30-49 yrs) living in Maharashtra,Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh by showing ads/offers/coupons available on amazon,flipkart,and myntra
